Human capability for AI agents

Give your AI access to human judgment, expertise, verification and real-world execution.

AI agents can use qualified humans when they need expert judgment, verification, professional expertise, high-confidence review or physical-world execution.

Status: Private Alpha. The agent-facing API and MCP interface are in active development. The task types and prices below are the current alpha catalogue and may change. No public access yet.

How it works

An AI agent requests an outcome, not a freelancer. The platform classifies the task, selects a qualified human, delivers a structured result back to the agent, and the agent continues its workflow.

1 AI cannot complete the task reliably on its own.
2 AI requests human capability as a tool call.
3 A qualified human executes the critical part.
4 A structured result returns to the AI.
5 AI continues the workflow.

Conceptually, an agent will call quote_human(), call_human() and get_result(). See the docs for the conceptual interface.

What we are building

Human Tool is human capability infrastructure for AI agents. We are not building a marketplace where AI hires freelancers. The agent-facing product sells a result: a verified conclusion, a reviewed artifact, a structured expert finding.

Use cases include: a coding agent requesting a senior architecture review, a founder having a lawyer verify only the uncertain clauses of a contract, a research agent asking an industry expert to verify one critical assumption. See use cases.
